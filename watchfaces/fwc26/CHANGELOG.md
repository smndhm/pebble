# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — Initial release

World Cup 2026 watchface with a custom typeface inspired by the tournament logo.

### Display modes

- **Logo** — two large minute digits, stacked
- **Logo Pixel** — same with a pixel-grid effect
- **Superposition Minutes** — minute digits overlapping diagonally
- **Superposition H+M** — two overlapping pairs, full hours and minutes
- **Classique** — standard 2×2 grid

### Customization

- 48 team presets with matching colors
- Manual color picker (background, two digit colors, overlap zone)
- Auto-detects your country at first setup

### Platforms

- All six Pebble platforms: aplite, basalt, chalk, diorite, emery, flint
- B&W platforms: white digits on black, checkerboard dither on overlap

### Settings UI

Available in French, English, Spanish, Portuguese and German.
